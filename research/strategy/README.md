# Strategy — Engineer ↔ CFO Refinement

A *cooperative* (not adversarial) two-role loop that converges on the best
real strategy for the orbital AI-inference data center. Unlike the
[debate/](../debate/) (which asked *should we do this*), this asks: *if we do
it, what is the best version?*

Two roles refine **one shared document** — [optimized_strategy.md](optimized_strategy.md):

- **Chief Engineer** — designs the most technically excellent, ambitious,
  cost-aware version. "If we engineer this for greatness, what does it look
  like?" Best architecture, best use of every lever (hot-loop, laser mesh,
  block-upgrade, deployable radiators, node design, constellation).
- **CFO** — reviews the engineering strategy and grounds it financially.
  Modifies it where the economics don't support the ambition; says where to
  spend and where to economize; aligns it with the investor reality (capital,
  crossover, premium, the build-to-learn arc).

## How it runs

1. The **Engineer opens** — writes Round 1 of `optimized_strategy.md`.
2. The **CFO reviews** — appends a round: what to keep, what to cut, what to
   re-scope, with the financial reasoning.
3. They alternate, each appending a round, **converging** on a strategy that is
   both technically excellent and financially grounded.

## Rules

- **Cooperative, not adversarial.** Shared goal: the best *real* strategy. Not
  "winning" — refining.
- **Every claim cites a source** — a project research doc or independent
  research. No unsourced assertions.
- **Honest.** The Engineer doesn't propose what physics/research rules out; the
  CFO doesn't cut what the economics actually support.

## Output

A converged `optimized_strategy.md` — the recommended build strategy. It feeds
the `feasibility-forge` conclusion document as the "best-case path."
