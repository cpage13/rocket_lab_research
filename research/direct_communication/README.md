# Direct Communications Workstream

*A second workstream in the Rocket Lab space-business feasibility project,
opened 2026-05-18. Sibling to the orbital data-center workstream
(`data_center/`); both share the project's research wiki.*

## The question

Can Rocket Lab build a **space communications** business (selling connectivity
to customers using a combination of **laser (optical)** and **broadband (RF)**
links) as a feasible standalone revenue line?

## The idea

- Orbital data centers (and other customers) need to move data. Rather than
  optical-over-terrestrial-fiber, route it **laser-to-laser in space**.
- Or: broadband uplink → laser relay in space → broadband downlink,
  connectivity that bypasses terrestrial constraints.
- Rocket Lab already owns relevant pieces, notably Mynaric optical terminals.
- This pairs naturally with the data-center workstream: data centers in orbit
  are a built-in customer for orbital communications, and vice versa.
